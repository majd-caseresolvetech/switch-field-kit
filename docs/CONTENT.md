## Scope

The original instructions, photographs, diagrams and credits remain in the downloaded PDF. This project supplies navigation, rendering and section labels. It does not replace the author or publisher.

The UK plant and fungus guides teach identification features. AUB Plants and People records Lebanese plant uses. None is presented as a complete edible-species key. AUB’s nursery manual is about propagation, and its water-testing guide is in Arabic. Specialist WHO/WEDC notes can require equipment and training.

The IFRC shelter PDF has damaged machine-readable text. Its original page images are intact. It uses section-title search.

## Adding a guide

Add a catalogue record with an official URL, author, edition, complete PDF hash and size, verified sheet count, scope and section mapping. Keep source content out of Git. The installer stops if the publisher changes the expected bytes. Recheck a replacement edition before updating the hash.
